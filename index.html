01 — About STCS

Perimeters, protected. Standards, exceeded.

STCS — Société Tunisienne de Clôture & Sécurité — supplies premium fencing and perimeter-protection products across Tunisia. From galvanised wire spooled by weight to fully welded green panels, every SKU is engineered to resist corrosion, tension and time.

We serve farmers reclaiming land, factories securing yards, municipalities lining highways, and homeowners protecting what they love. Our team handles specification, delivery and — when needed — installation.

  • Hot-dip galvanised steel, up to 4.9 mm gauge
  • PVC-coated panels in signature STCS green
  • Bulk pricing for professionals & installers
  • Same-week delivery across the Republic
